# Rehearsal: the nine scenes (2026-11-14)

Gold: `swipe` (review), `phone-touch` (receipts, funds), `hero-demo` and `static-card`'s pinned
window (simplefin, amazon, ai, reports, loans), `ruled-bento`'s terminal (install). Kept their
crops, touch and arrow cursors, `Collapse`/`Count`/`Roll`/`States` motion; changed everything else
to the app's look (`src/look.css`) and its own pages and words.

Beats, each `play="once"`, ending on its payoff with `act.mark('end')`:
review (swipe, throw, count) 6.4s · simplefin (paste, connect, accounts, count) 9.3s · receipts
(camera, read, items, match) 8.3s · amazon (sync, charge, items) 7.9s · ai (paste key, save, Review,
categorize, cost) 11.4s · reports (Food, Restaurants) 7.3s · funds (add, $500, move) 8.7s · loans
(pay extra, $5,000, owed and payoff roll) 8.6s · install (command, choose, domain, running,
sign-up page) 11.0s.

Captured: every scene's film at 640 wide (`.reel-stage`) and at 390 (`&bare`, the compact design),
both ending `rehearsal passed`; end stills at both widths; the whole video as a contact sheet
every 3s; the landing page full-page at 1440 and 390.

Film lines judged intentional:
- `touching` on a `Count` (the digit strip measured past its window): the kit's own part, nothing
  on the frames.
- `jump` on a field's or terminal's typed characters: typing feedback is instant.
- `settle snap` of a fraction of a pixel and `appears, then moves` on `Collapse`'s glide: the kit's
  motion, not visible at speed.
- `near aligned` between a browser's URL and the masthead's name: chrome and page, not one card.
- Terminal output lines carry `data-snap`: a terminal prints a line at once.

Not checked: dark mode (the scenes are light only, on purpose); hover; reduced motion's posters
(set to each payoff, not filmed); a fresh-eye review by a second reviewer; touch on a real phone.
Sampling gaps under load made every film `inconclusive` on coverage; the recorded video (stepped
frame by frame) was watched as a contact sheet instead.
