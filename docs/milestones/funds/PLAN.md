Funds: plan
==================================================================================================

> **Split in two (Kael, 2026-11-02):** "I've held an easy version of this milestone hostage to a
> hard version." Funds say what money is for, as a ledger of their own beside the accounts
> (where it is).

- **[A: Basic Funds](A/PLAN.md)** (built 2026-11-02, `v0.1.13`): the dual ledger with no rules. A fund
  fills or drains only when a transaction says so ("for Emergency", "from Emergency"), or by a
  move between funds. Funds hold dollars. Set on transactions, review cards, bulk actions,
  schedules and rules; a Funds page, each fund's page, and a home card.
- **[B: Complex Fund Functionality](B/PLAN.md)** (next): everything else designed so far.
  **Asset-aware funds come first** (Kael's guiding case: a brokerage whose four funds hold
  shares), then spend and fill rules, account defaults, groups (a house and its mortgage), budget
  funds and loans. B's working material: [the example book](B/example-book.md), [71
  scenarios](B/scenarios.md), and the verbatim discussion (`B/discussion.private.md`, git-ignored:
  it uses real figures).
